The [CTF writeups](/ctf/) section: a growing, structured archive of Capture the Flag
methodology. The point is not the flags, it is the reasoning, so each writeup follows the
same outline and keeps the dead ends in.

## Approach

- **Red-team learning methodology.** Enumerate, form a hypothesis, test it, and record
  what worked and what did not.
- **Consistent structure.** Challenge, event, category, difficulty, goal, initial
  observations, enumeration, failed attempts, breakthrough, solution summary, defensive
  takeaway, tools used, and lessons learned.
- **Defensive takeaways.** Where an offensive finding maps to a real-world defense, the
  writeup says so.

## Boundaries

- Legal, authorized practice only: everything targets CTF infrastructure or systems I own.
- No live flags and no fabricated placements. Results are only stated where they are
  verifiable.
- No guidance for acting against systems without authorization.

## Status

In progress. Writeups from this season's events (National Cyber League, BYU CTF, and
others) are being sanitized and published as they are finished.
