# Human review

This document is for a person, not a tool. Everything measurable has already been measured, and all four options pass. What is left is judgement, and that is the part a measurement cannot supply.

Please do not read `CONCEPT_COMPARISON.md` before answering. It states a preference, and knowing it first will change your answers.

## What to open

Compare four things, in whatever order you like. Try to spend real time on each rather than flicking between them.

Please look at each concept on a phone as well as a desktop, and try the Theme control in the header on at least one of them.

## Ground rules

- The content is identical across all three concepts and matches production. Any difference you see is design, not fact. If you spot a factual difference, that is a bug and worth reporting.
- Ignore the "design prototype, not the live site" tag in the footer.
- There is no correct answer. "None of these" and "B for this, D for that" are useful answers.

## The questions

**1. Which version makes the strongest technical evidence easiest to find?**

The strongest evidence is the AO-SINT writeup: a real competition result with a real screenshot and a real method. Starting from the homepage, in which version do you find it fastest, and in which version does it feel most clearly like the most important thing on the page?

**2. Which version looks least generated?**

Which one looks like a person made deliberate choices, and which one looks like it came out of a template or a model? Please say what specifically gives you that impression, even if it is small.

**3. Which version preserves a distinctive cybersecurity identity?**

Derek's site currently has a terminal flavour: the `~/derekwei.xyz` brand, monospace labels, a dark blueprint palette. Which version still reads as a security person's site rather than a generic portfolio, and which version has sanded that off?

**4. Which version is easiest to scan?**

Imagine ninety seconds before an interview, looking for certifications and a competition result. Which one gets you there with the least work?

**5. Does the numbered evidence rail help or distract?**

Concept B and Concept D both number their sections. B numbers them down a column on the left of every page. D numbers only the homepage, 01 through 05, under a horizontal rule. Does either help you understand where you are, or is it decoration?

**6. Does the full-width section rule clarify the layout?**

Concept D draws a line across the entire content width before each section. Concept B uses a short line instead. Does the full-width version make the page structure clearer, or does it make the page feel heavy and boxed in?

**7. Is AO-SINT given the right amount of prominence?**

In Concept D, AO-SINT gets a large image and a larger title, and the two other projects sit underneath as smaller text-only entries. Is that the right balance, too much, or not enough? Note that the two other projects have no landscape image available, only tall diagrams, which is why they are text first.

**8. Is the wider layout used effectively without making text difficult to read?**

Concept D uses more of a large screen than production does. Images and tables go wide, paragraphs stay narrow. On a large monitor, does that feel like good use of the space, or does the page feel stretched and disconnected?

## Also worth telling me

- Anything that looked broken, misaligned, or unfinished, and on which page and screen size.
- Anything that read as overclaiming. The content is meant to be defensible and evidence-linked, and a design that makes a modest fact look inflated is a real problem.
- Which one you would want to be judged by, if a recruiter opened exactly one of them.
- If your answer to any question is "production is still better", please say so. Keeping what exists is a legitimate outcome.
